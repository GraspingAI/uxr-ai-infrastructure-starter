# First Implementation Move

**State:** Proposed pilot  
**Owner:** Cyberdyne Research Lead  
**Review point:** After one completed readout pilot

## Start smaller than the strategy

The strategy describes a reusable system. The first move tests one workflow.

## Pilot

Use the **Research Readout Workflow** to create one internal readout answering:

> What does the current research responsibly support about first-use comprehension and confirmation in Auto-Actions, and what does it not establish about default enablement?

## Inputs

- `context/project-context.md`
- `sources/source-index.md`
- C-01, C-02, and C-08 as the initial source set
- C-03 for traceability
- C-04 and C-05 only if their different scope is useful to the question
- C-07 for decision context
- C-06 excluded as evidence

## AI role

- draft the structure;
- organize in-scope evidence;
- propose candidate wording;
- flag source links, scope differences, and open questions.

## Human review point

Before the readout travels, a named researcher checks every consequential claim against its source and chooses **keep / qualify / verify / remove**.

## Usefulness signals

After the pilot, ask:

- Could another researcher trace each consequential claim to its source?
- Did the workflow preserve important contradiction and scope?
- Did the model add unsupported claims or silently promote context into evidence?
- Was the review burden reasonable for the value produced?
- Which context, rule, or source-status information was missing or unnecessary?

## Next decision

Choose one:

- **expand** — reuse the workflow for another low-risk internal readout;
- **change** — revise the context, source index, workflow, or review rule and test again;
- **stop** — do not scale the workflow because the value does not justify the review or maintenance burden.

Do not expand to stakeholder self-service or higher-stakes decisions based on one successful pilot.
