# Decision Log

**Status:** Current example  
**Owner:** Cyberdyne Research

A decision log preserves what the team decided, why, and what evidence or constraints informed that decision. It is not a substitute for the research itself.

## Decision-record rule

A decision record should make clear:

- what was actually decided;
- which evidence informed it;
- which parts were professional or product judgment rather than direct research conclusions;
- who owns the decision;
- what would cause the team to revisit it.

## Why keep this separate?

Research findings and organizational decisions are related but not identical. Keeping the decision record distinct helps prevent a later reader from treating “the team decided X” as if the research itself proved X.
