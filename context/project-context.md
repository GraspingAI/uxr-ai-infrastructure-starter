## Scope

This fictional project concerns **Auto-Actions**, a Cyberdyne enterprise feature that can automate actions in a workflow.

The current product team is exploring whether Auto-Actions should be enabled by default for new enterprise accounts.

The current research question is narrower:

> What does the September 2026 research responsibly support about first-use comprehension, confirmation, and comfort with Auto-Actions, and what does it not establish about default enablement?

## Workflow goal

Use AI to help prepare planning material and stakeholder-facing research readouts from approved research sources while keeping evidence status, scope, contradiction, and human review visible.

## Stable background

- The September study included 8 current Cyberdyne enterprise users who had not used Auto-Actions before.
- The study focused on first-use comprehension during setup.
- It did not test long-term use, measure time savings in real work, or evaluate security, privacy, or compliance requirements.
- Other Cyberdyne research in the corpus includes older or differently scoped studies that may provide adjacent or contrasting evidence.
- Product leadership has a Q4 goal of reducing setup friction. That is organizational context, not user evidence.

## Human role

The researcher owns:

- framing the decision the research can inform;
- deciding which sources are in scope;
- interpretation of evidence;
- decisions about claim strength;
- contradictory or ambiguous evidence;
- whether an AI-generated claim is warranted enough to become a reviewed finding;
- final implications, recommendations, limitations, and stakeholder communication.

## Source rules

- Use the source index in `sources/source-index.md` before using the corpus.
- Source status matters. Searchability does not make all files equally authoritative.
- AI output is working material, not research evidence.
- Product decisions and stakeholder assumptions are context, not user evidence.
- If sources conflict, preserve the disagreement rather than silently reconciling it.
- Missing evidence should remain visible as a gap.
- Consequential claims should be traceable to the source or reviewed finding that supports them.

## Permitted use

Do not add participant identifiers, real-world notes or transcripts, confidential employer information, credentials, proprietary research material, or other protected data.

## Known limits
