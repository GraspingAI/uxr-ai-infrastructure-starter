# AI Operating Principles

**State:** Current worked example  
**Owner:** Cyberdyne Research  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-21

These principles govern the example kit. They are working rules, not universal policy.

## Operating Principle v1

> AI may help organize, transform, compare, and draft from approved research material. A researcher remains responsible for deciding what the evidence supports, how strong a claim may be, what uncertainty matters, and whether the output may travel.

## Supporting rules

1. **Candidate before finding**  
   Generated language is a candidate until it has been checked against the task, sources, and decision.

2. **Source status matters**  
   Reviewed findings, raw notes, adjacent research, AI output, and product context do not have the same evidentiary role.

3. **Review rises with consequence**  
   Review should increase when AI moves from transformation toward interpretation, confidence, causal explanation, recommendation, or consequential decision support.

4. **Do not hide contradiction**  
   When evidence disagrees or a different population points in another direction, preserve the difference.

5. **Not established is a valid answer**  
   Missing evidence should remain visible. Do not fill the gap with plausible language.

6. **AI output is not user evidence**  
   A generated synthesis, simulated participant, or synthetic dataset does not become evidence about real users because it is specific or coherent.

7. **Research and product decisions stay distinct**  
   Research can inform a decision without pretending to prove the decision itself.
