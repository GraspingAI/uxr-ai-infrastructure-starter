# Evidence and Synthetic Boundaries

**State:** Current worked example  
**Owner:** Cyberdyne Research  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-21

## Evidence boundary

For the September Auto-Actions question:

### May support a research claim

- **C-02 Reviewed Findings** — strongest current reviewed evidence from the September first-use study.
- **C-03 Research Notes** — may support traceability and inspection, but are not independently reviewed findings.
- **C-04 Prior Automation Study** — adjacent context only; different feature, older study, currentness not rechecked.
- **C-05 Admin Workflow Study** — useful contrasting evidence; different user group and workflow.

### May shape context, not user evidence

- **C-01 Study Brief** — scope, method, population, and limits.
- **C-07 Product Decision Memo** — organizational goals and open product questions.
- **C-08 Research Status Index** — source status and handling guidance.

### Does not independently support a research claim

- **C-06 AI Synthesis Draft** — generated candidate language, not reviewed evidence.

## Claim boundary

The current corpus can support claims about:

- first-use comprehension in the September study;
- observed hesitation around when Auto-Actions would act;
- variation in comfort after understanding confirmation;
- scoped comparison with adjacent or contrasting research.

It does **not** by itself establish:

- the correct default product setting;
- long-term adoption;
- time savings in real work;
- security, privacy, or compliance readiness;
- a universal user preference for more or less confirmation;
- behavior across all enterprise user groups.

## Synthetic-material rule

Synthetic material may be used to:

- test this workflow;
- rehearse a research session;
- prototype a readout;
- teach or train;
- generate edge cases for review;
- practice without confidential data.

Synthetic material may **not** be used by itself to establish what real users believe, need, prefer, or will do.

Label synthetic material clearly and keep it separate from reviewed research evidence.

## Privacy boundary

This public kit uses fictional material only.

Real participant data should enter an AI-supported workflow only when the organization has approved the system, access, consent, retention, and data-handling conditions for that use.
