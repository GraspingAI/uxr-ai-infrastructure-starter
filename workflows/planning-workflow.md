# Decision / Planning Workflow

**State:** Current worked example  
**Owner:** Cyberdyne Research  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-21

## Purpose

Turn a stakeholder request into a decision-relevant research plan before generating a study or readout.

## Example request

> “Can you tell us whether the research supports turning Auto-Actions on by default?”

## Workflow

1. **Name the actual decision.**  
   Example: whether product leadership should change the default enablement behavior for new enterprise accounts.

2. **Separate the decision from the research question.**  
   The current September study can inform first-use comprehension and confirmation expectations. It cannot by itself decide the default product setting.

3. **Name the evidence needed.**  
   Ask what evidence would be required to responsibly inform the decision: first-use comprehension, behavior after repeated use, value of confirmation across user groups, security/compliance constraints, adoption or workflow effects, and other relevant inputs.

4. **Inventory what is already known.**  
   Use `sources/source-index.md` to distinguish current direct evidence, adjacent evidence, raw notes, AI output, and product context.

5. **Make assumptions visible.**  
   Example: “Fewer confirmation steps will reduce setup friction without creating unacceptable uncertainty.”

6. **Name what is still unknown.**  
   Do not convert an unknown into a generated conclusion.

7. **Choose the next research move.**  
   The researcher selects the method and scope. AI may propose options and tradeoffs, but does not choose the final design.

8. **Set review and limits.**  
   State what the resulting study can and cannot establish before work begins.

## AI role

AI may:

- reorganize the stakeholder ask;
- propose candidate assumptions, risks, and evidence needs;
- compare plan variants;
- draft planning language.

The researcher owns:

- the decision framing;
- the evidence standard;
- method selection;
- tradeoffs;
- the final research question and scope.

## Output

A short planning brief containing:

- decision;
- current beliefs / assumptions;
- evidence needed;
- current knowns;
- unknowns;
- proposed next research move;
- limits;
- review point.
