# Synthesis Workflow

**State:** Current worked example  
**Owner:** Cyberdyne Research  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-21  
**Change rule:** Proposed changes should explain what problem they solve and should be reviewed before becoming the shared default.

## Purpose

Use AI to help organize patterns and tensions across approved research material without allowing the model to decide what the evidence warrants.

## Inputs

- `context/project-context.md`;
- `sources/source-index.md`;
- the specific synthesis question;
- only the corpus files that are in scope for that question.

## Workflow

1. **Name the decision or research question.** Do not begin with “summarize everything.”
2. **Confirm source scope and status.** Use the source index before retrieving material.
3. **Separate source types.** Keep reviewed findings, raw notes, adjacent studies, AI output, and product context distinct.
4. **Generate a candidate structure.** AI may organize patterns, tensions, exceptions, and open questions.
5. **Require traceability.** Consequential claims must point back to the source IDs that support or complicate them.
6. **Seek contradiction.** Include evidence that narrows or challenges the dominant pattern.
7. **Researcher review.** A researcher decides what becomes a finding, what stays inference, what needs qualification, and what is unsupported.
8. **Record gaps.** Missing support stays visible rather than being filled with plausible language.

## Output standard

A synthesis should make it possible to distinguish:

- direct evidence;
- reviewed finding;
- researcher inference;
- adjacent or contrasting evidence;
- organizational context;
- AI-generated candidate language;
- uncertainty and missing evidence.

## Stop conditions

Stop and return to the sources when:

- a consequential claim cannot be traced to evidence;
- source status is unclear;
- the workflow collapses different populations or studies into one claim;
- contradictory evidence has been flattened;
- the request asks the research to answer a decision the available evidence cannot support.

## What this workflow does not do

It does not decide which claims are warranted, replace researcher interpretation, turn AI output into evidence, or make a product decision.
