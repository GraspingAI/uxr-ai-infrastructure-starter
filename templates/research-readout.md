# Research Readout Template

**Status:** Reusable template  
**Owner:** Cyberdyne Research  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24

Use this template for reviewed research outputs. It is deliberately compact. Add detail only when the decision or audience requires it.

## Decision / question

What decision, question, or uncertainty is this readout meant to support?

## What we learned

State the strongest warranted findings in plain language.

For each consequential finding, include the source IDs or evidence trail that supports it.

## Evidence and interpretation

Distinguish:

- observed evidence;
- researcher interpretation;
- unresolved contradictions;
- missing evidence.

## What this does **not** establish

Name the important limits explicitly.

## Recommendation / next step

What should the team do next, if anything? Make clear whether this recommendation follows directly from evidence or includes professional judgment beyond it.
