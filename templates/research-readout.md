# Research Readout Template

**State:** Current  
**Role:** Reusable template  
**Owner:** Cyberdyne Research  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-26

Use this template to report reviewed findings, the evidence that supports them, their limits, and any decision implications. It is deliberately compact. Add detail only when the decision or audience requires it.

## Decision / question

What decision, question, or uncertainty is this readout meant to support?

## What we learned

State the strongest warranted findings in plain language.

For each consequential finding, include the source IDs or evidence trail that supports it.

## Evidence and interpretation

Distinguish:

- observed evidence;
- researcher interpretation;
- unresolved contradictions;
- missing evidence;
- any AI output used during synthesis from the reviewed finding itself.

## What this does **not** establish

Name the important limits explicitly.

## Recommendation / next step

What should the team do next, if anything? Make clear whether this recommendation follows directly from evidence or includes professional judgment beyond it.

## Permitted use

Record any sharing, reuse, or AI-system restrictions that another researcher would need to know.
