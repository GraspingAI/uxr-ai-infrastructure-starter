## Evidence rule

For the September Auto-Actions study:

- **C-02** is the strongest current reviewed evidence.
- **C-03** can help trace or inspect that evidence, but is not itself a reviewed synthesis.
- **C-04** and **C-05** can complicate or contextualize the story, but should stay visibly scoped to their different feature, population, or workflow.
- **C-06** is an AI-generated candidate, not evidence.
- **C-07** explains what leadership is considering, not what users believe.
- **C-08** helps determine source status.

## Example traceability

**Candidate claim:** “Users need a confirmation step before they will trust Auto-Actions.”

A responsible check would show:

- C-02 supports first-use uncertainty and shows that understanding confirmation is related to comfort.
- C-02 also records two participants who remained uncomfortable after understanding confirmation.
- C-05 provides contrasting evidence that experienced power users may value less confirmation in routine workflows.
- The corpus does **not** establish a universal preference or prove what the default product setting should be.

The chain is:

**source → AI candidate (if used) → researcher review → approved claim → stakeholder use**

## When to update this index

Update it when a source changes state, a new study enters scope, a file is superseded, or the team changes what material an AI-supported workflow may use.
